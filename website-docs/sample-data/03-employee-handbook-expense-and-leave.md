# Employee Handbook (Excerpt) · Expense Reimbursement and Leave

**Effective Date**: 2024-01-01
**Applicable To**: All regular employees of Nebula Technology
**Responsible Departments**: Human Resources Department · Finance Department

## 1. Travel Reimbursement

### 1.1 Transportation Expenses

| Rank | Air Travel | High-Speed Rail / EMU Train | Local Transportation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M3 and above | Economy Class | First Class | Reimbursed at actual cost, daily cap of 300 RMB |
| M1–M2 | Economy Class | Second Class | Reimbursed at actual cost, daily cap of 200 RMB |
| General Staff | Economy Class (requires approval 3 days in advance) | Second Class | Reimbursed at actual cost, daily cap of 150 RMB |

**Rules**:

- Air tickets must be booked through the company's designated business travel platform; reimbursement for self-purchased tickets must be submitted within 7 business days after the trip ends.
- For high-speed rail journeys exceeding 6 continuous hours, an upgrade to First Class may be requested, subject to email approval from the direct supervisor.

### 1.2 Accommodation Expenses

| City Tier | Nightly Cap (Tax Included) |
| --- | --- |
| Tier-1 Cities (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen) | 600 RMB |
| New Tier-1 Cities | 450 RMB |
| Other Cities | 350 RMB |

Amounts exceeding the cap are borne by the individual, unless uniformly arranged by the event organizer and specially approved by the Finance Department.

### 1.3 Meals and Allowances

- Meal expenses during business trips are **not reimbursed separately**; a daily travel allowance is provided instead:
  - Domestic Tier-1 cities: 150 RMB / day
  - Other domestic cities: 120 RMB / day
  - Overseas: per the Finance Department's monthly exchange rate table
- Client entertainment meal expenses follow the **business entertainment expense** process, separate from travel reimbursement.

## 2. Routine Expense Reimbursement

### 2.1 Submission Deadline

All invoices must be submitted via the OA reimbursement form **within 30 days of the expense date**. Overdue submissions require a joint explanation signed by both the direct supervisor and the Finance Manager.

### 2.2 Common Expense Categories

| Category | Description | Required Attachments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office Supplies | Unit price < 500 RMB | Invoice + Purchase List |
| Training Fees | External certification, industry conferences | Invoice + Training Notice + Completion Certificate |
| Test Equipment Purchases | R&D test prototypes, sensors | Invoice + Project Number + Asset Registration Form |

**R&D personnel** purchasing hardware such as smart home hubs or sensors for testing must first create an "R&D Prototype" entry in the asset system before linking it to the reimbursement form. Refer to the *Product Manual · Smart Home Hub Pro* for the model and specifications to fill in.

## 3. Annual Leave and Time Off

### 3.1 Annual Leave Days

Calculated based on length of service (based on hire date):

| Length of Service | Annual Leave Days |
| --- | --- |
| Under 1 year | 5 days |
| 1–10 years | 10 days |
| Over 10 years | 15 days |

Employees hired before March 31 of the current year receive annual leave prorated for the remaining months of that year; those hired after this date begin receiving full annual leave the following year.

### 3.2 Leave Request Process

1. Submit a leave request in OA in advance, selecting the type (annual leave / sick leave / personal leave, etc.).
2. Within 3 days: approved by the direct supervisor; 3–7 days: approved by the department director; over 7 days: recorded with HR.
3. Sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days requires documentation from a hospital rated Level 2 or above.

### 3.3 Conflicts with Project Schedules

If a leave period overlaps with a finalized release milestone (e.g., firmware 3.5 canary release, knowledge base POC acceptance), it must be communicated in the project group chat, and a **work handover contact** must be designated. Leave requests related to Zhang Ming's team's POC require additional notification to Product Director Chen Hao.

## 4. Handling of Violations

- False invoices, duplicate reimbursements: once verified, the funds will be recovered and handled according to the *Employee Reward and Punishment System*.
- Unapproved overseas business travel: expenses will not be reimbursed.
